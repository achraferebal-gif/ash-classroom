About Ash's Classroom

A growing collection of practical English learning activities designed for classrooms, teachers and independent learners.

Why this site exists

Ash's Classroom was created to make English practice easier to run and more enjoyable to use. The focus is on activities that can be opened quickly and used immediately in a real lesson.

The site combines classroom games with structured practice for speaking, grammar, vocabulary and IELTS.

The goal: useful activities with clear instructions, minimal setup and enough flexibility to work with different levels.

What you'll find here

  • Interactive classroom games
  • Speaking prompts and challenges
  • Grammar explanations and practice
  • Topic-based vocabulary review
  • IELTS speaking practice
  • Teacher-focused resources

For students and teachers

Students can use the activities for extra practice, while teachers can project them in class for warm-ups, review, pair work and team competitions.

The free section will continue to grow. Additional teacher resources, larger content packs and premium materials may be introduced later.
